
# Welcome to the Bitblog

## **About Me**

![['/content/Tech and AI/Using Mistral to Setup My Quartz Blog for Free/Attachments/Pasted image 20260219134739.png'|300]]

My name is **Darius Bittle-Dockery** and I’m a **medical anthropologist turned experience designer**, obsessed with:

- **The power of mindful attention** (what takes your attention makes your reality).
- **Tech that empowers people** (not the other way around).
- **Building systems that serves people** (from refugee health to banking UX).

Currently, I work at PNC on a human-centered design and research team, but this blog is where I explore the **unfiltered, experimental side** of my work and life. Everything on the BitBlog should be considered my own viewpoint or writing unless otherwise specified by a representative or publication.

I am also a [Mistral Ambassador](https://docs.mistral.ai/ambassadors) and support their [vision and mission](https://mistral.ai/about) of building "a future where AI is abundant and accessible" by doing my part through this blog to democratize "artificial intelligence through open-source, efficient, and innovative AI models, products, and solutions."

## **Why Bitblog?**
This is a space for personal and professional exploration where I document my journey to understand technology, the self, and the systems that shape our lives. No gatekeeping. No jargon. Just **real learning, real questions, and real experiments** in:
- **Tech for Humans**: AI, self-hosting, and tools that empower (not exploit).
- **Self-Expansion**: Mindfulness, discipline, and the art of *doing less to do more*.
- **Systems Thinking**: How design, infrastructure, and culture shape our world.

### What You'll Find Here
- **AI for Non-Techies**: Practical guides to using Mistral, Ollama, and other AI software and tools.
- **Life Chronicles**: Updates on my personal projects and experiments.
- **Designing for Humans**: Case studies on inclusive design, tech, and systems.
